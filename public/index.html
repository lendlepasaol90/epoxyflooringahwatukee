A flooring crew that starts with the concrete, not the color chart

Most of the floors we are asked to fix in Ahwatukee did not fail because of a bad color choice. They failed because somebody rolled a coating onto dusty, sealed or damp concrete and hoped for the best. Within a summer or two the floor lifted under the tires, yellowed by the garage door or turned chalky where the afternoon sun hits it.

So our work starts underneath. We look at the slab, check for moisture, mark the cracks and figure out what is already on the surface. Then we grind it, repair it and pick a coating system that fits how you use the space. A garage that holds a truck, a golf cart and a workbench needs something different from a casita floor or a salon on Ray Road.

We install garage floor coatings, residential epoxy for interior rooms, decorative flake and metallic epoxy finishes, fast-curing polyaspartic and polyurea systems, patio and pool deck coatings, and floors for commercial spaces and warehouses. Every estimate is free, done in person, and written down so you can compare options side by side.

Garage floors that handle 120-degree afternoons

The garage is the hardest-working floor in most Ahwatukee homes, and the hottest. With the door closed in July, the air inside can run well past 110 degrees, and the slab soaks up that heat for hours. Tires roll in hot, drip coolant or pool chemicals, and every monsoon storm blows fine dust under the door seal.

A good garage system shrugs that off. Ours typically pairs a penetrating epoxy or polyurea base coat with a decorative flake broadcast and a UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat. The base grabs the ground concrete, the flakes hide dust and add grip, and the topcoat resists hot tires, chemicals and the sun that hits the first few feet inside the door.

  • Flake blends from tight, speckled granite looks to bold, full-coverage chips

Residential epoxy beyond the garage

Coated concrete has moved indoors in a lot of Arizona homes. It stays cool underfoot, it cleans with a damp mop, and it does not trap dust and allergens the way carpet does. Homeowners ask us about it for casitas, laundry rooms, home gyms, mudrooms off the garage, game rooms and full open-plan living areas where the tile is coming up anyway.

Interior floors get a different approach than garages. We pay closer attention to sheen, color consistency across large areas and how the floor reads next to cabinetry and wall color. Satin finishes hide footprints better than full gloss, and a sealed metallic floor can carry a whole room on its own. See our residential epoxy flooring page for room-by-room advice.

Decorative and metallic finishes

Not every floor needs to look like a garage. Decorative systems use the same tough resins, but the finish becomes the design. Metallic epoxy uses suspended mica pigments that we move by hand while the resin is wet, so the floor ends up with depth and drift that looks like polished stone, poured marble or moving water. No two are the same, which is the point.

Flake floors are the practical cousin. Vinyl chips broadcast into the base coat give a speckled, terrazzo-like texture that hides dust and wear. In Ahwatukee, warm blends with tan, cream and a little charcoal tend to sit well next to stucco and desert landscaping.

Heat, sun and monsoon dust: what Ahwatukee floors deal with

Floors here face a specific set of problems. Garage slabs heat up all day and cool overnight, which works the concrete and anything bonded to it. Low western sun pours through open garage doors and turns cheaper epoxies amber. Summer storms push fine dust and water under doors, and pool chemicals, fertilizer and coolant end up on the floor sooner or later.

Frequently asked questions

The things Ahwatukee homeowners ask before they book. Something else on your mind? (480) 555-0142 and ask.

How long before I can park in my garage again?

It depends on the system. With a polyaspartic topcoat, most garages are walkable the same evening and ready for cars in roughly 24 to 48 hours. A full epoxy build needs longer, usually around three days before vehicle traffic and about a week for full chemical cure. We give you the exact window for your floor before we start, and in July we usually beat it because heat speeds the cure.

Will the coating peel when I pull in on a hot afternoon?

Peeling under hot tires, usually called hot-tire pickup, happens when a thin coating sits on a slab that was never properly opened up. Tires coming off a 115-degree street soften cheap coatings, and the bond lets go. We diamond-grind every slab so the coating soaks into clean, porous concrete, and we use base coats and topcoats rated for that kind of heat. That combination is what stops the peeling, not a thicker layer of paint.

Do you work on floors that already have a coating?

Yes. If an older coating is failing, we grind it off down to sound concrete before anything new goes down. If it is well bonded and only worn, sometimes it can be scuffed and recoated, which saves money. We test it on the first visit and tell you which situation you are in.

What about cracks and the control joints in my slab?

Hairline and settlement cracks are cleaned out and filled with a repair material before coating. Control joints, the straight cuts in the slab, are usually honored, meaning we keep them visible so the floor can still move a little without cracking the coating. Some homeowners prefer them filled for a seamless look; we will talk through the trade-off with you.

Is an epoxy floor slippery?

A bare high-gloss coating can be slick when wet, especially with dusty monsoon water tracked in. Flake floors already have some texture, and we can broadcast a fine anti-slip additive into the topcoat to add grip without making it hard to mop. Tell us who uses the space, kids, older relatives, a dog, and we will set the texture accordingly.

Does my HOA need to approve a garage floor coating?

Interior garage floors usually do not need approval, but driveways, front walks and anything visible from the street often do. Check your CC&Rs before you commit to a color for an exterior surface. We are happy to provide product names and color samples for your architectural review submission.

How much does a garage floor coating cost?

Price depends on square footage, slab condition, the system you choose and how much repair work the concrete needs. A two-car garage with a sound slab and a standard flake system is very different from a three-car garage with old paint to remove and a cracked apron. We measure and look at the slab in person, then give you a written price per system so you can compare.
