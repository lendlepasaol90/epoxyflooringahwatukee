Areas we serve around Ahwatukee

Based in the Foothills, covering the southeast Valley for residential projects and further for commercial work.

Travel time matters on coating jobs. Resin systems have working windows, especially in summer, and a crew that is close by can start early and react quickly if weather shifts. That is why we keep our residential work concentrated around Ahwatukee and the communities that border it.

If your town is not listed, give us a call. We take on commercial and warehouse projects across a wider area and will tell you straight away if we are not the right fit.

Ahwatukee Foothills

Our home turf. From the older homes around the Lakes and Lakewood to the larger garages in the Foothills, Club West and Mountain Park Ranch, we know the slabs, the HOA rules and the summer heat these floors face.

Tempe

South Tempe homes along the Kyrene corridor and around the lakes are minutes away across the I-10. We also coat shop floors and small commercial units in Tempe business parks.

Chandler

West Chandler neighborhoods, garages near the Price corridor and light commercial spaces. Chandler homes often have newer slabs that are ideal candidates for fast polyaspartic systems.

Phoenix

South Mountain Village and central Phoenix projects by appointment, including older homes with painted or sealed garage floors that need full removal before coating.

Laveen

Newer construction on the west side of South Mountain. New slabs need time to cure before coating, so we will help you time the work after you move in.

Gilbert

Residential garages, home gyms and workshops. Many Gilbert homeowners pair a flake garage floor with a coated patio or side yard.

Guadalupe

Homes and small businesses right next to the Foothills, where we often handle crack repair and coating together.

Mesa

West Mesa shops, warehouses and homes. Commercial and industrial jobs here often call for high-build epoxy or polyurea with striping.

Sun Lakes

Golf cart garages, lanais and patios. We often recommend a lightly textured topcoat here for steady footing.
